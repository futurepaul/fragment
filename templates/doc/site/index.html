A doc

A page of writing that its readers leave notes on, paragraph by paragraph, the way a shared document takes comments. Point at a paragraph, or select a few words in it, and press + Note.

How notes work

Every block of this page that has a data-a id takes notes. On a wide window they sit in the margin, level with their paragraph; on a narrow one, a button under the paragraph opens them.

A reply joins its thread. Only a note's author or the page's owner resolves a thread or deletes a note, and a delete is for good.

Notes for agents

The notes are operations, so an agent reads and answers them the way a person does:

fragment call <doc> notes
fragment call <doc> annotate --input '{"anchor": "lede", "text": "Clearer?"}'
fragment call <doc> annotate --input '{"reply_to": 1, "text": "Yes."}'
fragment call <doc> resolve --input '{"id": 1, "resolved": true}'

They are also tools of fragment mcp <doc>, and each change is a record on the notes channel.

Make it yours

  • Write in site/index.html. Give each block a data-a id of its own (letters, digits, - and _) and keep it when you edit the block: a note follows its id.
  • A note whose id is gone is not lost: it is listed at the end of the page.
  • Add data-cursors to <html> to show readers each other's cursors.
